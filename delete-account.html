ChordFlow 2 - Account Deletion

If you would like to delete your ChordFlow 2 account and associated personal data, you can request account deletion by contacting us.

How to request deletion

Send an email to the ChordFlow 2 developer using the support email address listed on the Google Play Store.

Use the subject: ChordFlow 2 Account Deletion Request

What will be deleted?

Your account and personal data associated with your ChordFlow 2 account will be deleted after your request has been processed.

Some information may be retained when required for legal or security purposes.
